About OlaDigitalHub

Data doesn't run HR. People do. Data just tells them the truth faster.

OlaDigitalHub was built on one observation: most HR teams have more data than they use and fewer answers than they need. The gap isn't a tool problem. It's a translation problem.

Why "Fractional People Analytics Partner"

Companies between 50 and 2,000 employees are usually too small to justify a full-time Head of People Analytics, and too data-heavy to run HR on instinct. OlaDigitalHub sits in that gap: a part-time analytics partner who builds the dashboard, reads it every month, and tells leadership what it means.

That's a different job from "someone who builds dashboards." A dashboard is a deliverable. A partner is accountable for the answer.

How we work

Every engagement starts the same way: one HR question the business can't currently answer with data. From there we build backwards — the data model, the dashboard, and the monthly report exist to answer that question, not to look impressive in a demo.

Tools are Power BI and Excel by default, because that's what most finance and leadership teams already trust. SQL and Python come in when the question needs them, not by default.

What guides the work

Three rules OlaDigitalHub doesn't break

01 — Start with the question

No dashboard gets built until we can name the decision it's meant to inform. "It would be nice to know" is not a brief.

02 — Numbers need a narrative

A chart without a sentence next to it gets ignored by boards. Every report ships with the "so what," not just the "what."

03 — Anonymize by default

Case studies, samples and templates use anonymized or synthetic data. Client data stays client data — always.
